# Video Checklist

## Rules (from the official rules)
- ☐ **Under 3:00** (judges aren't required to watch past 3 minutes)
- ☐ Shows the project **actually working** on the device it was built for
- ☐ Uploaded to **YouTube, Public**, link pasted on Devpost
- ☐ **No third-party trademarks** or copyrighted music without permission → anonymized display ON, no music or royalty-free only
- ☐ English (captions on)

## Before recording
- ☐ Click **Reset demo**
- ☐ Anonymized display ON; API Trace panel visible
- ☐ Browser 1440×900, zoom 100%, clean profile (no personal tabs/bookmarks)
- ☐ Sandbox buyer credentials ready (never show your real PayPal)
- ☐ Rehearse once with `DEMO_SCRIPT.md`
- ☐ Quiet room; test mic level

## Recording tips
- Record screen and voice separately if easier (screen first, voiceover after).
- Keep the cursor slow and deliberate. Pause one beat on the wow moment.
- Show real sandbox IDs in the API Trace panel during the checkout.

## After
- ☐ Watch it once fully; check length
- ☐ Add captions (YouTube auto-captions, then fix product names)
- ☐ Title: "AgentShelf — PayPal AI Hackathon"
