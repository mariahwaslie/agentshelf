Rehearse `DEMO_SCRIPT.md` against the app.

1. Start the app in replay mode (`make demo`), anonymized display ON, click Reset demo.
2. Walk every scene in order. For each: take a screenshot at 1440×900, look at it, and note anything confusing, slow (> 2 s), ugly, misaligned, or showing a real brand name/secret/personal info.
3. Time the whole walkthrough.
4. Report a table: scene, OK/problem, fix suggestion. Do not fix anything unless asked.
